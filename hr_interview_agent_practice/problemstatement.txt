Problem Statement
AI-Powered HR Employee Support & Automation Agent

Business Context

A multinational company with 50,000+ employees across multiple countries receives thousands of HR-related requests every day.


Employees contact HR for:

Leave requests
Payroll issues
Reimbursement claims
Employment verification letters
Policy clarification
Internal transfers
Promotion inquiries
Exit process support

The current HR team is overwhelmed and response times often exceed 48 hours.

The company wants to build an AI-powered HR Support Agent capable of handling employee requests autonomously while ensuring compliance with company policies, security standards, and labor regulations.

Your Task

Design an AI Agent System that:

Understands employee requests

Examples:

My salary credit is incorrect.

Can I transfer to the Bangalore office?

Generate my employment verification letter.

I want to resign.
Executes HR workflows

Examples:

Raise reimbursement ticket

Generate documents

Transfer requests

Exit requests
Maintains employee context

Escalates when required
